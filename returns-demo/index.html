Grading step

In
Reason code, comment, inspector note, photos, SKU spec.
Out
likelihood category rationale, schema-validated.
Idempotent
Cached on rma_id. A re-run never re-grades or re-bills.
On failure
Routes to human review. Never guesses toward a vendor claim.

The join everything depends on

A return knows its SKU, not its production batch. Resolved by order date against receipt windows. This is the most fragile link in the system and the first thing I would check against real data, because wrong batch attribution means wrong vendor claims.

Where it deliberately stops

No photo, no claim
Gradeable from text, but never auto-billed to a vendor.
Unknown batch
Counts in quality totals, held out of the ledger.
New reason codes
Alerts instead of silently defaulting to a bucket.
Claim reconciliation
Claimed against received. A widening gap means a vendor is ignoring you.
Duplicate RMAs
Deduped so one unit is never claimed twice.
Low confidence
Anything ambiguous goes to a person, not to a number.

Not built here: source connectors, Drive filing, the persistent ledger, weekly sends, escalation hooks. That is the full build.
