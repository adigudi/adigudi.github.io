Case Study · Featured

Video Game Market Intelligence Platform

5,950 games. 17 years of sales. Where should a publisher actually launch?

5,950Games analyzed
17 yrsOf sales data
51%NA share of market
+7.6 ptsEU share growth

What the data said

NA leads, EU is catching up

NA accounts for 51% of all units, but EU share rose +7.6 pts between 2004–08 and 2010–14.

Action by volume, Shooter by momentum

Action wins total units, but Shooter's share more than doubled (12% → 22%) — the genre to bet on.

The market is blockbuster-driven

Custom C++17 K-means surfaced 5 clusters — ~93% of titles are "modest sellers"; hits carry the slate.

Boxed-console sales decline post-2008

Exp-smoothing forecast with confidence bands — partly real shift to digital, partly data-recency. Pair with digital data.

Architecture

Raw Data 2 CSVs · 17 yrs sales + global population
→
14-Step Pipeline Python ETL · SQLite warehouse · C++17 K-means · scikit-learn · XGBoost · stats tests
→
Serving FastAPI (12 endpoints) · Streamlit · Tableau · Docker · CI/CD

Stack

Python 3.11 C++17 SQLite FastAPI Streamlit scikit-learn XGBoost SciPy Tableau Docker GitHub Actions pytest
Build a Shooter. Weight the launch toward Europe.

That's where the data points: Shooter share more than doubled while Sports/Racing declined, and EU is under-tapped at 0.13 copies/person/year vs NA's 0.31 — momentum and headroom in the same direction.
